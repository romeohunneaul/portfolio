I design screens with Claude. As a product manager I need two things at once. The prototype
has to sit on the real product: its components, data shaped like its own, flows and scenarios
as tangled as the real ones. And it has to be fast: three UX ideas compared this afternoon, not
next sprint.

The tools I tried each pick one side. Claude Design, Lovable and Figma Make are fast, but
they bring their own components and their own data; in my tests of Claude Design (October
2026), every state and every scenario was faked by hand. A dev branch and Storybook have the
real thing, but every change is slow, and versions never sit side by side.

<ProtolabJourney />

So <Highlight>I built protolab</Highlight>. It has its costs: it needs the app's repo to run, and a link leaves my
machine only once the lab is deployed. But of everything I tried, it is the best balance
between the real product and speed.
